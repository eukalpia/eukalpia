### EDU AI · Inside CAU University's LMS

An AI-powered assessment assistant, fully integrated into the university's learning platform. It turns course topics, documents, presentations and recorded lectures into ready-to-use tests — helping teachers prepare assessments and students receive clear feedback within the existing LMS workflow.

- Generates single-answer, multiple-answer, true/false and fill-in-the-blank questions.
- Transcribes audio and extracts teaching material from uploaded documents and presentations.
- Checks answers, calculates scores and explains the correct answers.
- Supports assessment creation and completion in English and Russian.

Private implementation. [Discuss the project ↗](https://t.me/umaraliev_ravshan)
